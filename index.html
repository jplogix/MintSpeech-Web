FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about text-to-speech engines, platforms, and what Mint Speech is designed to do.

What is Mint Speech?
Mint Speech is a premium text-to-speech app for macOS. Turn any text into natural speech with Edge TTS, Gemini, or local LLMs, then bookmark passages, export MP3s, and revisit readings in history—all reachable via quick shortcuts.
Which TTS engines does Mint Speech support?
Mint Speech supports Edge TTS, Gemini, and local LLMs that run on your device. Switch engines and voices to match the content you are listening to.
Which platforms are supported?
Mint Speech is currently available for macOS. Windows, iOS, and Android versions are in development.
Can I use quick shortcuts from other apps?
Yes. Mint Speech is built for global access via quick shortcuts—select text anywhere, trigger a shortcut, and listen without switching context.
Is Mint Speech free?
Check the Mac App Store page for current pricing and available options.
Can I save and export what I listen to?
Yes. Bookmark passages you want to keep, browse recent readings in history, and download audio as MP3 for offline listening.
Does Mint Speech replace professional speech or accessibility support?
No. Mint Speech is a text-to-speech and listening workflow tool. It does not replace guidance from a qualified professional or platform accessibility features.
When will other platforms be available?
Windows, iOS, and Android versions are currently in development. Release timing will be shared when available.
